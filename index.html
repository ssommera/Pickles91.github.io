Three of them

EEG decoding · deep learning

Liminal EEG — predicting experience intensity from brain recordings

Built an end-to-end pipeline in MNE and PyTorch over 34-subject DMT EEG recordings: ICA artifact removal, epoching, and Welch power-spectral-density features. Benchmarked an EEGNet convolutional model and a temporal Transformer against a published SVM baseline under leave-one-subject-out cross-validation, so scores reflect generalization to unseen people rather than memorized subjects.

Data 34 subjects Set 4,273 × 120 Models EEGNet · Transformer Eval LOSO CV Stack MNE · PyTorch

Medical imaging · research assistantship

3D prostate MRI segmentation with nnU-Net v2

Trained and evaluated 3D segmentation models on Task05 of the Medical Segmentation Decathlon as a CS research assistant at Winona State. Work covered preprocessing multi-modal MRI volumes, configuring nnU-Net v2 training, and reading the resulting overlap metrics against the reference annotations.

Security ML · poster, Winona RCA 2024

Decision trees for remote-to-local intrusion detection

Compared decision-tree detection of R2L attacks trained on KDD Cup 1999, on NSL-KDD, and on the two combined. Merging the datasets raised accuracy to 99.93% with a 91% detection rate and a 0.08% false-alarm rate, beating either dataset alone. Presented at the Winona State Research and Creative Achievement Celebration.

Accuracy 99.93% Detection 91% False alarm 0.08% Data KDD99 + NSL-KDD
